Any other list, and every paragraph, heading or block around it, is left exactly as Markdown produced it. Because Markdown ends a list at a plain paragraph, a line of prose between two dialogues splits them into two: {% dialogue(names="S=Socrates; C=Christopher; V=Visitor; Y=Young Socrates") %} - S: Christopher!
