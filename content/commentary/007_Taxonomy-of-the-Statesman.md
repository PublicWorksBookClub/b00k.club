The visitor from Elea accounts for 'statesman' by following the same pattern he establishes a moment prior, in Plato's _[Sophist](/works/plato-sophist/)_. He creates a taxonomy by finding divisions by which to group things and in the dialogue performs a kind of search of the tree that forms.
